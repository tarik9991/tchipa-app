How it works
1. Create your profile (name, phone, email) and your PIN.
2. Top up your balance with a Tchipa agent.
3. Pick your items, enter your delivery address and pay with your balance.
4. Track your order to your door.

The Tchipa balance can only be used to buy items in the Tchipa shop: it is not a bank account or a payment method usable elsewhere, and it cannot be transferred to another user.
