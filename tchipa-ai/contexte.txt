== 1. WHAT IS TCHIPA (the main app) ==========================================

Tchipa is a mobile app that turns crypto (USDT) into a real VIRTUAL CREDIT CARD
(Mastercard or Visa) that you can use to pay on any website online — from Algeria,
with no bank account and no KYC.

- No bank needed. No document, no ID verification (no KYC).
- Instant: your card is generated in under 2 minutes.
- Works worldwide online: Amazon, AliExpress, Temu, Netflix, Shopify stores, PayPal
  top-ups, subscriptions — anywhere that accepts Mastercard / Visa online.
- Built for Algeria: you pay in Algerian dinars (DZD) via BaridiMob (Poste / Barid
  IMob mobile transfer), and Tchipa handles the crypto side for you.
- The card is virtual (a number + expiry + CVV shown in the app). There is no plastic
  card; it is used for online payments only.

Tagline: "Your virtual card, activated instantly. No bank, no KYC, no limits."


== 2. HOW TO GET A CARD (step by step) =======================================

1. Download the app:
   - Android: go to tchipa.co.uk and tap "Download APK".
   - iPhone: on tchipa.co.uk tap "Open on iPhone" — open it in Safari, then
     Share → Add to Home Screen (it installs as an app, no App Store needed).

2. Choose how much you want on the card (the card amount in USD).
   - Mastercard: from 5 USD up to 499 USD.
   - Visa: from 5 USD up to 1000 USD.

3. Contact a Tchipa agent (a real person, USDT reseller in Algeria) — usually via
   Telegram. You tell them the card amount.

4. Pay the agent in Algerian dinars (DZD) via BaridiMob, at the day's exchange rate.
   - The rate changes daily. It is around 242 DZD per 1 USD (indicative — the app
     shows the live "Square" market rate on the home screen).
   - There is a small activation fee of about 7 USD per card, included when you order.

5. The agent then sends the exact amount of USDT (on the Polygon network) to the
   address Tchipa generated for your order.

6. Within about 2 minutes, the card appears inside your Tchipa app: card number,
   expiry date and CVV. You can start paying online immediately.

The agent handles the crypto for you — you only deal in dinars via BaridiMob. You do
NOT need to own or understand crypto to use the main Tchipa app.


== 3. WHO IS A "TCHIPA AGENT" ================================================

A Tchipa agent is a REAL PERSON — a USDT reseller in Algeria — not a bot and not an
automated Telegram service. You pay the agent in dinars via BaridiMob; the agent sends
the equivalent USDT to your card order. Coordination (price of the day, BaridiMob
proof, card delivery) happens directly, usually on Telegram.

To become an agent yourself, you use the Tchipa app in "Agent mode" (in the app:
Profile → enter the agent PIN). Agents buy/resell USDT and serve clients.


== 4. PRICES, RATES AND LIMITS ==============================================

- Exchange rate: about 242 DZD per 1 USD, but it CHANGES EVERY DAY. The app shows the
  live parallel-market ("Square") rate. Always confirm the day's rate with your agent.
- Activation fee: about 7 USD per card.
- Card amount range: Mastercard 5–499 USD, Visa 5–1000 USD.
- Network fees: payments use USDT on Polygon, which has ultra-low fees and instant
  confirmation (no expensive gas).
- Tchipa does not charge hidden monthly fees; you pay for the card amount + the
  activation fee, in dinars, at the day's rate.


== 5. SECURITY ============================================================

- Current app version is v4: protected by a client PIN, email verification, and
  theft-proof card claiming (a card is tied to your phone number / order, so someone
  else cannot grab it).
- No KYC: your card is linked to your order, not to your personal identity.
- USDT runs on the Polygon network (fast, cheap, secure).


== 6. AFFILIATE / REFERRAL PROGRAM (earn 1%) ================================

Tchipa has a referral (affiliate) program:
- You earn 1% of the amount loaded on every card created by people you refer.
- Get your personal referral code in the app: Profile → the green "Parrainage / earn 1%"
  button. Share your code; when a new user enters it and buys cards, you earn 1% of each.
- Automatic on-chain payout: once your earnings reach 5 USDT, Tchipa automatically sends
  your USDT to a Polygon address you register in the app (protected by your PIN).
- There is a safety daily cap of 50 USDT per day on automatic payouts.
- Earnings are credited automatically each time a referred card is actually issued.


== 7. TCHIPA WALLET (the second, separate app) =============================

Tchipa Wallet is a DIFFERENT app from the main Tchipa card app. Do not confuse them:
- The main Tchipa app = makes virtual cards to pay online.
- Tchipa Wallet = a self-custody crypto wallet to store, send and receive USDT.

Tchipa Wallet details:
- Self-custody / non-custodial: YOU own your funds. The app creates a 12-word secret
  recovery phrase (seed). Tchipa never holds your money or your keys. If you lose your
  seed phrase and PIN, no one can recover your funds — back up the seed safely.
- Holds and moves USDT and POL on the Polygon network (chain 137).
- Send / receive ERC-20 tokens, scan/show QR codes for addresses.
- Locked by a 6-digit PIN, with optional fingerprint / biometric unlock.
- Gas loan feature: if you have USDT but no POL to pay network gas, the wallet can lend
  you a tiny amount of POL so you can send, and it is automatically repaid in USDT. This
  means you can move USDT even with zero POL.
- Shows the live "Square" exchange rate (DZD) on the home screen, like the main app.
- Download: on tchipa.co.uk tap "Download Wallet" (Android APK).

Use Tchipa Wallet if you want to hold your own USDT. Use the main Tchipa app if you
just want a virtual card to pay online.

- "How do I earn money with Tchipa?" Through the 1% referral program, or by becoming a
