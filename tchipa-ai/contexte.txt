== 1. WHAT IS TCHIPA ========================================================

Tchipa (also "Tchipa DZ") is an online CLOTHING SHOP for Algeria.
- Clothes come directly from manufacturers in China, at factory prices.
- They are delivered to the customer's home in Algeria.
- DELIVERY AND CUSTOMS ARE INCLUDED in the price shown in the app. Nothing
  more to pay on arrival (no shipping fee, no customs fee).
- The customer pays in ALGERIAN DINARS through BaridiMob (no bank card needed).
- Website: https://tchipa.co.uk — orders are placed in the Tchipa app.


== 2. CATALOGUE =============================================================

- More than 7,700 models for women, men and children: dresses, t-shirts,
  pyjamas, children's shoes, etc.
- Available now: WOMEN (Femme), MEN (Homme), CHILDREN (Enfants).
- Coming very soon: HIJAB and ABAYA sections (not available yet).
- Each product shows its photos, colours, sizes and its price delivered in
  Algeria. The price can be different for each colour/size (variant).
- The website shows the best-sellers; the full catalogue is in the app.


== 3. HOW TO ORDER (5 steps) ================================================

1. Install the Tchipa app.
   - Android: download the APK from the website (button "Télécharger l'APK").
   - iPhone: open the web version of Tchipa in Safari, then
     Share → "Add to Home Screen" (Sur l'écran d'accueil). It then opens like
     an app.
2. Top up your Tchipa balance: contact a Tchipa agent and send them the amount
   in dinars via BaridiMob. The agent credits your Tchipa balance in the app.
3. Choose your items in the catalogue (colour + size), then pay with your
   balance.
4. Tchipa buys the item from the supplier. The parcel goes through our
   partner warehouse in China, then is shipped to Algeria.
5. Follow your order in the app until delivery.

The exchange rate (dinars ↔ balance) is given by the agent at the moment of
the top-up; never state a fixed rate.


== 4. DELIVERY & TRACKING ===================================================

- Indicative delay: usually 2 to 4 WEEKS between the order and the delivery.
  It depends on the supplier and on shipping — it is not a guarantee.
- Route: supplier in China → partner warehouse in China → shipping to
  Algeria → delivery at home.
- Order statuses in the app:
  Payée (paid) → Achetée (bought from supplier) → À l'entrepôt (at the
  warehouse in China) → Expédiée (on its way to Algeria) → Livrée (delivered).
- OUT OF STOCK: if the supplier no longer has the item, the order is
  cancelled and the balance is REFUNDED AUTOMATICALLY. The customer can then
  choose another item.


== 5. SIZES =================================================================

- Chinese sizes are SMALLER than European/Algerian sizes.
- Take 1 to 2 sizes ABOVE your usual size. Example: you usually wear M →
  take L or XL. For a loose/oversize fit, rather 2 sizes above.
- If in doubt, ask your Tchipa agent for advice before ordering.
